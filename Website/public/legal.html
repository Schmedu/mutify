What you are buying

A download of Mutify for macOS — a signed, notarized disk image containing the app. It is a one-off purchase with no subscription and no upgrade fee. It needs macOS 14 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac; it will not run on an Intel Mac.

Mutify's source code is published under the MIT licence at github.com/Schmedu/mutify, and the same build is available there at no charge. Paying here is voluntary support for the work, not the only way to obtain the software. 0,99 € is the minimum; anything above that is your choice.

Delivery and the right of withdrawal

Delivery is immediate: the download link appears as soon as Stripe confirms the payment. By starting that download you agree to delivery beginning at once and acknowledge that this ends the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal for digital content (§ 356 Abs. 5 BGB).

That is the legal position. The practical one: if Mutify is not what you wanted, email e@uffelmann.me and you get your money back, no questions and no deadline.

Payment and VAT

Payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. Card details are entered on Stripe's own checkout and never reach this site — it never sees, stores or transmits them. Prices are shown with VAT included; the rate applied is the one for your country, calculated by Stripe, and your receipt itemises it.

Privacy

The app. Mutify has no account, no server and no analytics. Your networks, your devices and your settings stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Mutify on your Mac. Nothing about your location or your networks ever leaves the machine, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

This website. No cookies, no tracking, no analytics. The page is static; the two functions behind it talk to Stripe and to GitHub's release API and to nothing else. Vercel, which hosts it, keeps standard access logs (IP address, page, timestamp) for operating the service. Google Fonts serves the two typefaces, which means your browser contacts fonts.gstatic.com.

When you buy. Stripe receives the data it needs to take the payment, including your email address and the billing country used for VAT. It is the controller for that data under its own privacy policy. I can see the resulting payment records — amount, country, email — in the Stripe dashboard, and keep them as long as German tax law requires (ten years, § 147 AO). Nothing is passed on to anyone else, and there is no mailing list.

To ask what is stored about you, or to have it removed as far as tax law allows, email e@uffelmann.me.
